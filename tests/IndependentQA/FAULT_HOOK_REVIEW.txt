Execution distinctions

Adding a QA-owned driver that injects existing callbacks is distinct from changing production hooks, but its exact handshake and oracles must be coordinated with QA. No original QA test, expected result or Developer regression was changed here.
Process interruption (including Environment.Exit or an external process kill) does not prove physical power-loss, controller-cache or storage-media durability. This review makes no such claim.
