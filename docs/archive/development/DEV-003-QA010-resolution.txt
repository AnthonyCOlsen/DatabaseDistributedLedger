QA coordination / exact checkpoint contract

Location: src/UserDimensions.Ledger/Management.cs, Import's canonical candidate loop,
after ImportBuffered returns and its result/durable counts have been recorded,
before processing the next candidate.

  batch-grant-durable:<input-index>:<full-signed-block-sha512>
    Fires for a newly durably accepted GRANT_MASTER in a DDLB import. It does not
    fire for a duplicate grant. The grant has completed the existing durability
    procedure and reclassification; no next dependent candidate has begun.

  batch-item-completed:<input-index>:<full-signed-block-sha512>
    Fires after a parsed candidate is processed, including duplicates or rejected
    candidates. It does not fire for unparsed/malformed framing items, unprocessed
    items after an interrupted operation, or ordinary raw single-block imports.
    Its result/counts are complete, but the final CLI response is not yet emitted.

Indices are zero-based ORIGINAL TRANSPORT indices, not canonical processing ordinals.
For an inverted [dependent, grant] DDLB, the grant is processed at input index 1;
the checkpoint carries index 1 even though it is reached first. Callback order for
two newly durable valid items is grant-durable:1, item-completed:1, item-completed:0.
Signed-block SHA-512 is over exact signed bytes, not the DDLB wrapper.

FI-07 and FI-15 -- remaining blockers, deliberately not simulated

Location: src/UserDimensions.Ledger/Persistence.cs, DurableFiles.Move, the existing
MoveFileExW call with flags 8 (MOVEFILE_WRITE_THROUGH) and optional 1 (replacement).
The existing after-rename callback is after successful return. It cannot observe
an internal final-rename/pre-directory-durability interval. Configuration replacement
uses the same operation. The Win32 call has no managed intermediate callback at
these exact requested boundaries. Adding a callback after return would mislabel
the observation, and removing WRITE_THROUGH or inventing an unqualified separate
flush would change the persistence procedure. No such change is made here.

FI-07 and FI-15 remain BLOCKED pending Developer/QA/Owner coordination on a safe,
observable qualification approach. No new durability guarantee or physical power-loss
claim is made. Existing before-rename, after-rename, after-flush and after-publish
checkpoints remain unchanged. FI-09 and FI-09-GRANT now have executable actual-batch
observation points; independent QA must adapt its own driver and rerun them.
